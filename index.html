GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I’m Assiyam Inaz, a poet who finds beauty in the things left unsaid. My words explore love, longing, and the quiet spaces between words. Through poetry, I give a voice to feelings that are often difficult to confess. I write to turn unspoken stories into something that can finally be felt, understood, and read.

My journey as a writer is only beginning, but every poem, every character and every story brings me one step closer to the writer I dream of becoming.

Still writing. Still dreaming. Still becoming. ♡
